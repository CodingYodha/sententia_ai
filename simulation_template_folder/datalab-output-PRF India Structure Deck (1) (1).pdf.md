## Overview of Transaction ---

![Blue stylized logo consisting of two overlapping geometric shapes forming a square-like structure.](c803f6f6e2c49429d2951832bd0f208d_img.jpg)

The transaction is structured to enable PRF and ESW to jointly support the development of the **Jireh School** by investing into Devaraj Educare through two separate channels.

Under this model,

- PRF establishes an Indian Wholly Owned Subsidiary (WOS)
- ESW invests directly into Devaraj.

Both entities subscribe to CCPS/equity Shares and enter into parallel consulting and oversight arrangements with Devaraj.

The structure creates a **dual-investor, dual-contract model** where:

- 1. PRF (via its WOS)** contributes capital for school infrastructure, long-term capacity building, and educational oversight.
- 2. ESW** invests directly into Devaraj to support strategic growth and quality outcomes.
- 3. Devaraj Educare** serves as the main operating entity that receives investments from both parties, governs school management, and deploys funds into the Jireh School project.

Through separate Shareholders' Agreements (SHAs) and Consulting Agreements, both PRF's WOS and ESW obtain governance rights, reporting obligations, and operational visibility over the development of the school. The goal is to ensure transparent deployment of funds, structured construction and operational monitoring, and sustainable long-term growth of the Jireh School.

## Step 1 | Investment by ESW in Devaraj India

### Structure & Observations:

Given that Devaraj India would either be engaged in education or construction development of educational institutions, 100% FDI would be permitted, but ESW would only be permitted to invest in FDI-compliant instruments issued by Devaraj India, which are equity shares, compulsorily convertible preference shares (“CCPS”), compulsorily convertible debentures (bonds), partly paid shares and share warrants. Equity shares are the simplest for transaction execution.

### Key Points to Finalise:

1. How much will ESW invest in Devaraj India ?
2. How many equity shares will Devaraj India issue to ESW? What shareholding percentage will ESW aim for in Devaraj India?
3. Will ESW want any shareholder rights in relation to Devaraj India such as a board seat or veto matters.
4. Devaraj India will need to obtain a registered valuer valuation report before issue of shares to ESW and will need to follow the private placement rules, including holding a board meeting, shareholders’ meeting for super-majority approval, and then a board meeting for allotment to ESW.
5. Devaraj India to issues shares on private preferential basis and report the FDI through its AD Bank in India within 30 days from the date of issue of equity shares to ESW.
6. We need to know who Devaraj India uses as its Indian AD Bank and explain the structure to them so that they understand, and we pre-fill out all of the documents for the FDI reporting in Form FC-GPR.

- Devaraj Educare Private Limited (“**Devaraj India**”) is an Indian private company limited by shares, and its main business object is to establish, own, run, operate, manage, develop and administer on its own through a fully equipped educational institution. It also operates Jireh School in India.
- Educational Services Worldwide, LLC (“**ESW**”) is a limited liability corporation incorporated in the United States of America.
- ESW proposes to fund Devaraj India by investment in fresh equity shares issued to ESW. This foreign direct investment transaction would be regulated by Indian foreign exchange law (“**FDI Rules**”). The per share price paid for the equity shares will need to be at least (if not higher than) the fair market value per share price determined by an Indian valuer who delivers a valuation report on the shares (“**FMV Valuation**”) of Devaraj India . The investment must be through normal banking channels and into a bank account that Devaraj India opens with its Indian AD Bank.

## Step 2 | Incorporation of India NewCo by Project Rescue Foundation

### Structure & Observations:

To incorporate the WOS, PRF will need to have nominate at least 2 directors, of which 1 must be an Indian resident. They can nominate 2 foreign residents and 1 Indian residents, so that the board has a majority of USA-resident nominees to pass decisions at their board meetings. These nominee directors will need to have a director identification number (DIN) and digital signature certificate (DSC) before being appointed as directors – which takes 1 or 2 weeks from receipt of the necessary documents in the correct form. Our team can help with this and provide the related checklist of documents and steps.

The WOS will also need at least 2 shareholders since Indian law requires private companies to have at least 2 shareholders. One of them can be a nominee shareholder which holds a small number of shares (e.g. 10 / 100 shares). Equity shares are simplest for initial incorporation. The initial share capital infused in the WOS can be minimal – for example INR 100,000 divided into 10,000 equity shares of INR 10 each.

PRF will need to choose an Indian AD bank to open for capitalising the WOS and to open an Indian bank account for the WOS.

### Key Points to Finalise:

1. We will need to finalise the actual business that the WOS will undertake. Consulting in education may be the simplest since consultancy services would allow PRF to acquire 100% under the automatic route of the WOS.
2. WOS will need to ensure that its financial assets do not constitute more than 50% of the total assets (netted off by intangible assets) and income from financial assets do not constitute more than 50% of its gross income, to ensure it does not require prior registration and approval with the Reserve Bank of India as a non-banking financial company (NBFC).

- Project Rescue Foundation (“**PRF**”) will incorporate an Indian wholly owned subsidiary which will be an Indian private company limited by shares (“**WOS**”) (*we can help with the incorporation*).
- The WOS incorporation will need to comply with the Companies Act, 2013 and the FDI Rules.
- The WOS incorporation process will require KYC documents which will need to be notarized and apostilled in the US and the entire process typically takes a few weeks after receiving the necessary documents in the correct form. Our team can help and send the necessary checklist of documents for incorporation.
- At incorporation, only certain basic licenses such as PAN and TAN (direct tax registrations) are issued, but depending on future business, the WOS will need to apply for indirect tax registration (GST registration). Our team can help with this registration. Other licenses relating to employment or offices can be analysed and assisted with once we have a brief on what business the WOS will conduct.

#### Core Idea

- PRF and ESW consolidate their participation through a **single U.S. entity (Delaware JV LLC)**.
- The JV LLC owns **100% of NewHold India (WOS)**, which becomes the **sole FDI investor** into Devaraj Educare.
- A unified channel replaces the earlier multi-pathway model, reducing complexity and regulatory exposure.
- This structure ensures **clean governance, aligned incentives, and streamlined compliance** across all layers.

#### How the Money Flows

- PRF and ESW invest into the **JV LLC** in the U.S.
- The JV LLC capitalizes **NewHold India** through a single, well-documented FDI inflow.
- NewHold India invests into **Devaraj Educare** via CCPS.
- Construction funds move through a **milestone-based escrow mechanism**, ensuring disciplined and transparent deployment.
- Only **one valuation, one SHA, and one FC-GPR process** apply, simplifying the entire transaction lifecycle.

- NewHold India holds **all investor rights** under a unified Shareholders' Agreement with Devaraj.
