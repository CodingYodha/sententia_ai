PRESS NOTE 3 (2020 SERIES) — FDI FROM LAND BORDER COUNTRIES
Government of India — Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)
Issued: April 22, 2020 | Effective: Immediately upon notification

AND

PRESS NOTE 2 (2026 SERIES) — AMENDMENTS AND CLARIFICATIONS
Effective: 2026 (exact date subject to gazette notification)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PRESS NOTE 3 (2020) — FULL TEXT SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BACKGROUND:
Press Note 3 of 2020 was issued by DPIIT to curb opportunistic takeovers and acquisitions
of Indian companies during the COVID-19 pandemic, targeting primarily Chinese investments.
The amendment revised the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-Debt Instruments) Rules 2019.

CORE PROVISION — PARAGRAPH 3.1.1(a):
A non-resident entity can invest in India, subject to the FDI Policy. However, an entity
of a country, which shares a land border with India, or where the beneficial owner of an
investment into India is situated in or is a citizen of any such country, can invest only
under the Government Route.

LAND BORDER COUNTRIES (as defined):
Countries that share a land border with India for purposes of this Press Note:
1. China (People's Republic of China)
2. Bangladesh (People's Republic of Bangladesh)
3. Pakistan (Islamic Republic of Pakistan)
4. Nepal (Federal Democratic Republic of Nepal)
5. Myanmar (Republic of the Union of Myanmar)
6. Bhutan (Kingdom of Bhutan)
7. Afghanistan (now governed by Taliban administration)

NOTE: Sri Lanka does NOT share a land border with India and is NOT subject to this restriction.
UAE, Singapore, Mauritius, Cayman Islands, USA, UK, EU member states are NOT land-border
countries and are NOT subject to this mandatory government approval requirement.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCOPE AND COVERAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SECTION A — NEW INVESTMENTS:
All new FDI from entities in land-border countries, across ALL sectors (including those
otherwise on the Automatic Route), now requires prior government approval through DPIIT.
There is no threshold exemption — even USD 1 of investment requires approval.

SECTION B — TRANSFER OF EXISTING INVESTMENTS:
Any transfer of ownership in an existing Indian company which would result in beneficial
ownership moving to a land-border country entity requires prior government approval.
This includes:
- Sale of shares to a land-border country entity by an existing shareholder
- Corporate restructuring that changes ultimate beneficial ownership to land-border country
- Pledge or hypothecation of shares in favor of a land-border country entity

SECTION C — DOWNSTREAM INVESTMENT:
If a company with land-border country beneficial ownership makes downstream investment in
another Indian entity, such downstream investment also requires government approval regardless
of the sector.

SECTION D — BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP TRACING:
The Press Note applies based on BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP, not immediate investor jurisdiction.
The following chain must be analyzed:

Example: Situation requiring Government Approval:
  Mr. Zhang Wei (Chinese national) → 100% owns HoldCo (Singapore SPV) → 74% invests in
  Indian Company (Technology sector)
  
ANALYSIS: Although the immediate investor is a Singapore company (not a land-border country),
the BENEFICIAL OWNER is Mr. Zhang Wei, a Chinese national / PRC-resident entity. Therefore,
Government Approval is REQUIRED even though Singapore is not a land-border country.

Example: Situation NOT requiring Government Approval:
  Ms. Sarah Chen (US national, not PRC national/resident) → 100% owns Cayman Fund →
  Singapore SPV → 49% investment in Indian Technology company
  
ANALYSIS: The beneficial owner is a US national. Government Approval NOT required.
Automatic Route applies (subject to sectoral cap compliance).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DEFINITION OF "BENEFICIAL OWNER" FOR PN3 PURPOSES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For corporate entities: The natural person(s) who ultimately own or control the entity,
either through direct shareholding exceeding 25% (later reduced to 10% under SBO rules),
or through exercising effective control.

For funds: The General Partner/Investment Manager and/or ultimate fund investors
based on ownership/control thresholds.

The Ministry of Finance / FEMA rules cross-reference Companies Act Section 90 thresholds
for SBO determination. Under revised rules, the threshold for UBO determination for
PN3 purposes is 10% effective economic ownership.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
GOVERNMENT APPROVAL PROCESS (PN3 INVESTMENTS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AUTHORITY: Foreign Investment Facilitation Portal (FIFP) — www.fifp.gov.in
PROCESSING: Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) for investments above INR 5,000 crore;
  competent ministry otherwise
TIMELINE: 60 working days from date of complete application
DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Detailed ownership structure up to natural person level
- Business plan and purpose of investment
- Declaration of no security threat
- Annual report of investing entity
- Declaration that no national security interest is compromised

PRESS NOTE 2 (2026) — AMENDMENTS AND CLARIFICATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Press Note 2 of 2026 Series made the following key amendments to PN3 (2020):

1. ENHANCED UBO TRACING METHODOLOGY:
   The threshold for triggering PN3 Government Approval route through indirect ownership
   is clarified as follows:
   - Direct ownership ≥ 10% by land-border country entity/national → Government Approval required
   - Indirect ownership through multi-layered structures → aggregate economic interest ≥ 10%
     triggers Government Approval requirement
   - Control through rights (board seats, veto rights, call options) even below 10% economic
     threshold → fact-specific analysis required, DPIIT may require Government Approval

2. LOOK-THROUGH FOR INVESTMENT FUNDS:
   For foreign portfolio investors (FPIs) and investment funds, PN2 (2026) requires look-through
   to underlying investors where any single land-border country investor holds ≥ 10% of
   fund units/interests.

3. ANNUAL CERTIFICATION:
   Indian companies with foreign investment are required to file annual certification
   (with each FC-GPR filing) confirming that no beneficial owner from a land-border country
   holds ≥ 10% economic interest, directly or indirectly.

4. EXEMPTIONS:
   - Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) registered with SEBI where no single land-border
     country investor exceeds 10% → exempt from individual approval (fund-level compliance applies)
   - GIFT City investments → relaxed provisions under IFSC framework
   - ODI (Outbound Direct Investment) by Indian entities → PN3 does not apply

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STRATEGIC STRUCTURING IMPLICATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

COMMON MISCONCEPTION: Many investors assume routing through Singapore or Mauritius avoids
PN3 Government Approval requirement. This is INCORRECT when the ultimate beneficial owner
is from a land-border country. The Government Approval requirement follows the beneficial
owner, not the immediate investor.

VALID STRUCTURE (if investor is genuinely non-land-border): 
UAE investor → Singapore SPV → India: NO PN3 issue (UAE is not land-border; applies
Singapore-India DTAA benefits)

INVALID ATTEMPT TO AVOID PN3:
Chinese investor → Singapore nominee SPV → India: PN3 APPLIES. Government Approval required.
Using Singapore structuring does not circumvent PN3 when beneficial owner is Chinese.

PERMITTED STRUCTURE FOR PN3-COVERED INVESTORS:
Chinese investor WITH Government Approval → Singapore SPV (post-approval) → India:
Investment permitted upon obtaining prior government approval, regardless of routing.
The approval covers the full structure including SPV layers.
