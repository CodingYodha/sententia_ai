COMPANIES ACT 2013 — SECTION 90: SIGNIFICANT BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP (SBO)
AND RULES MADE THEREUNDER
Source: Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Government of India
Companies (Significant Beneficial Owners) Rules 2018, amended 2019 and 2021

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. STATUTORY PROVISION — SECTION 90
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Section 90(1): Every individual who, acting alone or together, or through one or more
persons or trust, including a trust and persons resident outside India, holds beneficial
interests of not less than 10% of shares in a company, or who has right to exercise, or
actually exercises, significant influence or control as defined in clause (27) of section
2, over the company (herein referred to as "significant beneficial owner") shall make a
declaration in the prescribed form and manner to the company specifying the nature of
his interest and other particulars, as may be prescribed.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. DEFINITION OF SIGNIFICANT BENEFICIAL OWNER (SBO)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Under the Companies (SBO) Rules 2018 (as amended), an individual is a SBO if they hold
individually or jointly, through any indirect means:

THRESHOLD: 10% or more of:
(a) shares in the reporting company, OR
(b) voting rights in the reporting company, OR  
(c) right to receive dividend or any other distribution from the reporting company

OR

CONTROL THRESHOLD: Right to exercise or actually exercises significant influence or control
(as per Section 2(27) of Companies Act 2013 — control means right to appoint majority of
directors or to control management or policy decisions)

INDIRECT HOLDING — HOW TO CALCULATE:
Shares held through a body corporate: Individual's % in body corporate × body corporate's
% in reporting company = Indirect interest of individual in reporting company

Example: Individual holds 60% in a Singapore company, which holds 20% in Indian company.
Individual's indirect interest = 60% × 20% = 12% → EXCEEDS 10% threshold → SBO declaration required.

3. OBLIGATIONS OF SBO — DECLARATION REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FORM BEN-1 (Declaration by SBO to Company):
- The SBO must file Form BEN-1 with the reporting Indian company
- Filed WITHIN 90 DAYS of commencement of SBO Rules (for existing holdings)
- Filed WITHIN 30 DAYS of any change in SBO status or % holding
- Contains: SBO's name, address, nationality, PAN/passport number, nature of interest,
  date of acquiring interest, % shareholding/voting rights

FORM BEN-2 (Return of SBO by Company to Registrar of Companies):
- The Indian company must file Form BEN-2 with the ROC (Registrar of Companies)
- Filed WITHIN 30 DAYS of receiving BEN-1 declaration from SBO
- Alternatively, if no BEN-1 received, company must independently identify SBOs
  and file BEN-2 with details obtained through due diligence

FORM BEN-3 (Register of SBO maintained by Company):
- Company must maintain a register of SBOs in prescribed format
- Available for inspection at registered office
- Entries must be updated within 14 days of any change

4. OBLIGATIONS OF COMPANY — NOTICE TO SHAREHOLDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Section 90(5): A company may, by notice in writing, require any member of the company
(whether he holds shares in his own name or as a trustee) to give details of any interest
in the shares of the company held by such member, whether for himself or for any other person.

The company can issue notices in Form BEN-4 to shareholders suspected of being nominees
or holding on behalf of others, requiring disclosure of the beneficial owner.

DUTY OF NOMINEE SHAREHOLDERS:
Any person holding shares on behalf of another must disclose the identity of the beneficial
owner to the company. Failure to respond to BEN-4 notice within 30 days entitles the company
to apply to NCLT to restrict rights attached to such shares.

5. PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SECTION 90(10) — Individual SBO fails to make declaration:
Penalty: INR 50,000 and in case of continuing default, further fine of INR 1,000 per day
(maximum INR 2,00,000 total)

SECTION 90(11) — Company fails to maintain register or file BEN-2:
Penalty: INR 10,00,000 (ten lakh rupees) and every officer in default: INR 1,00,000
In case of continuing default: additional INR 1,000 per day up to INR 5,00,000

SECTION 90(12) — Person makes false declaration knowingly:
Imprisonment up to 1 year OR fine up to INR 25,00,000 OR both

6. INTERACTION WITH FDI REGULATIONS AND PRESS NOTE 3
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The SBO framework under Companies Act interacts critically with FDI Policy:

PRESS NOTE 3 THRESHOLD CALIBRATION:
Press Note 3 (2020) uses the concept of "beneficial owner" to determine whether
land-border country entities are investing in India. The SBO Rules provide the
definitional framework for determining beneficial ownership.

KEY INTERACTION:
If an individual from a land-border country (e.g., China) holds ≥ 10% directly or
indirectly in an Indian company, BOTH requirements apply:
1. Companies Act §90 SBO declaration (Form BEN-1/BEN-2) to MCA/ROC
2. Press Note 3 Government Approval from DPIIT for the FDI structure

DISCLOSURE TIMELINE FOR INBOUND FDI TRANSACTIONS:
At CLOSING:
  - FC-GPR filing with RBI (within 30 days of share allotment)
  - BEN-1 by SBO to Indian company (within 30 days if new SBO threshold triggered)
  
Within 30 days of BEN-1 receipt:
  - BEN-2 filing by Indian company with ROC

7. PRACTICAL GUIDANCE FOR CROSS-BORDER INVESTMENT STRUCTURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MULTI-LAYERED STRUCTURES:
For structures where investment comes through multiple SPVs (e.g., Singapore HoldCo →
Mauritius SubCo → India OpCo), the SBO analysis must pierce through all layers to
identify the natural person with ≥ 10% economic interest in the Indian company.

Each layer of the structure must be included in the BEN-1 declaration, showing the
chain from the natural person UBO down to the Indian company.

FUNDS AND POOLED VEHICLES:
For investment through a private equity fund or venture capital fund:
- If any single investor in the fund holds ≥ 10% of the fund AND the fund holds ≥ 10%
  in the Indian company → that investor may be an SBO of the Indian company
- Fund manager must assess investor base for potential Indian SBO obligations
- Disclosure risk assessment is standard part of India-bound fund investment process

SOVEREIGN WEALTH FUNDS AND GOVERNMENT-OWNED ENTITIES:
Government entities, sovereign wealth funds fully owned by foreign governments may
be exempt from SBO requirements in certain circumstances — consult specific guidance
from MCA. However, FDI policy compliance remains applicable.
